# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want everyone who interacts with this project to feel welcome and
respected. Whether you're opening an issue, submitting a pull request, or
asking a question, we expect you to treat others with kindness and
patience — and we'll do the same for you.

## Our standards

**Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:**

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what's best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior can be
reported to the project maintainer by opening a private issue (use the
"Feature request" template and mark the title with `[Private]`) or by
emailing **twinmugume@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed and handled
with discretion.
